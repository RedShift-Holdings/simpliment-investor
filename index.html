    01 — The Timing Thesis

    A closing window, and the assets already built to take it.

    WordPress still powers roughly two-fifths of the web. Its owners are drowning in overlapping SaaS subscriptions and want one integrated, own-your-data suite on infrastructure they already run. The opportunity is to win that installed base now — before the slow migration off WordPress accelerates. The product is built; the capital buys national reach.

    This is not a bet on a founder building software. Twenty assets are already shippable. The raise funds one thing: getting to market at national scale, fast — sales, marketing, and finishing the industry verticals to MVP.
    One control plane

    Anyone can build a CRM. The moat is the coherent system.

    SS Hub deploys, licenses, themes, and AI-brokers across every module and every customer site from one place — a genuine operational moat that is expensive and slow to replicate, whatever AI does to the cost of code.

    03 — The Agency Channel

    One agency, an entire book of client sites — from one seat.

    The 8–12-subscription sprawl that hits one WordPress owner hits an agency once per client, multiplied across their whole roster. Fleet collapses that into one managed stack across every site the agency runs — the single biggest channel multiplier in the model.

    Fleet readiness

    Deploy anything, manage everything

    Individual installs and multisite networks, mixed, from one console — health, version, and package status for every managed property at a glance, with one-click push-to-fleet updates instead of logging into each site.

    Agency control

    One Hub, every client site

    Role-based access per site, per-client branding via the shared Theme/Chrome token system, and centralized module management — an operator sees and acts on the whole roster without context-switching between logins.

    Agency pricing

    Priced on managed sites, not seats

    Power $50 · Performance $100 · Professional $125 per managed site, plus bolt-ons — the same mechanism as the free 7-site tier, just a bigger pool. Deployment count is a contract lever, not a different product: one site or a hundred, same tech.

    Scalable with AI

    More sites per operator-hour

    A shared, BYOK AI broker at the Hub level powers AI-guided setup and AI site-building (describe a page, it's built) across the whole fleet. As AI absorbs the repetitive setup and build work, one agency operator services more client sites per hour — the leverage compounds with roster size.

    Pulse — one CMO-style snapshot across every client account, urgent decisions ranked automatically
    Hub — scans a fresh install and skips everything already done; minutes, not hours, per client
